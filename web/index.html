    How it works

    1. The die names a colour: that is the tile you must lay, and the other colour plays it.
    2. Then one pawn action, if you want one: settle onto the tile you just laid, step, or attack across a diagonal.
    3. Every area — two or more tiles of one colour touching edge to edge — scores for whoever holds most of it.
    4. A tile is worth 2 points, 3 if the area matches its owner's colour, but only 1 in the largest area, which is taxed. A pawn left in the reserve costs 6.

    Anarchchess

    The house rules the community plays, collected from r/AnarchyChess, anarchychess.org and the Anarchchess bots. Every rule is a switch: turn it off and it simply does not exist.

    Anarchess

    An implementation of the published game by Dimitris Grammenos (FORTH). The die chooses the tile colour; the player of the opposite colour opens, then every turn lays a tile and may use one pawn action. In SOLO the prescribed pawn action is required when one is available. Areas of two or more same-coloured tiles score for whoever holds the most pawns. Choose the standard table, the one-player 192-point SOLO challenge, or jump-and-chain-capture Anarcheckers in the Anarchess tab.

    About this build

    The engine, the Anarchess rules and the Anarchchess rules running in this page are the same Python files the desktop application uses, executed by Pyodide. Nothing is rewritten for the browser, and after the first visit everything is cached: it works on a plane.
